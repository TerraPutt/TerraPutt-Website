What an installation looks like

A permanent installation —
not a product you unbox.

TerraPutt is made to order for your property and commissioned on site. Here is what that involves, end to end.

01
Assessment
We visit and read the site — grade, drainage, sun, sightlines — and agree on a footprint, from a compact practice green to a full short-game surface.
02
Design
We design the green's resting form and the set of configurations you want on call, tuned to your game and to the architecture of the yard.
03
Installation
It's built in as permanent landscape and commissioned in place. Lead times are measured in months, not weeks — this is construction, not delivery.
Why this exists

A green that changes its mind — and a category that follows.

Every other serious system in a home — light, climate, sound, water — became something you could shape on command. The ground never did. It stayed poured, planted, and fixed.

TerraPutt is the first step past that: an outdoor surface that reconfigures on demand. A putting green now, a wider language of responsive landscape after it. We're building the first ones by hand, for a small number of homes.
